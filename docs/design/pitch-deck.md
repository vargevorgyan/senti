# Pitch deck

How to build Senti slides (for Claude Design, Slides or by hand).

- **Background:** cream (`surface`) for most slides; one or two espresso (`ink`) section slides for contrast. Never terracotta as a full-slide background except the closing slide.
- **Cover:** the app icon, "Senti" in Bricolage Grotesque, one line: "A watchdog for your AI agents." A bark wave in sage as the only decoration.
- **Titles:** `headline` style in ink; one idea per slide, written as a sentence ("Agents act. Senti checks first.").
- **Numbers:** key stats in terracotta at 48–64px, with a short DM Sans caption in `ink-muted`.
- **Product slides:** show the real alerts (Blocked, Ask) from the components section on a cream slide; they explain the product faster than diagrams.
- **Diagrams:** espresso lines, cream boxes, terracotta only for Senti's own box in the flow (User → Agent → Senti → Computer).
- **Margins:** 48px (`space-12`) on every side, left-aligned text.
- **Avoid:** stock security imagery, more than one terracotta element per slide besides numbers, gradients, emoji.
